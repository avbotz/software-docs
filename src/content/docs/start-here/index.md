AVBotz is a student-run underwater team based at Amador Valley High School. We compete with collegiate and high school teams at the international [RoboSub Competition](https://robosub.org/).

Our current vehicle is **Marlin V3** and it primarily runs a codebase called [**Thalassic**](https://github.com/avbotz/thalassic/).

1. [The big picture](/start-here/big-picture/)
2. [What to learn](/start-here/what-to-learn/)
3. [Set up your computer](/start-here/setup/)
4. [Run the simulator](/start-here/run-the-simulator/)
5. [ROS 2, hands on](/start-here/ros-2-hands-on/)
6. [Tour of the code](/start-here/code-tour/)
7. [Pick an area](/start-here/pick-an-area/)

Then do the [tutorials](/tutorials/first-mission/): write a mission, a ROS 2 node and some C++, and open your first pull request.

The [Concepts](/concepts/coordinates/) pages explain the ideas underneath: coordinates, sensors, control, behavior trees and computer vision.

- **Look up unfamiliar words** in the [glossary](/help/glossary/) or with the search box.
- **Stuck?** Check [Troubleshooting](/help/troubleshooting/) or ask in the software Discord server.
- **Fix incorrect or confusing information** with the "Edit page" link at the bottom of every page. It can be [your first pull request](/tutorials/first-pull-request/).
